# Real OTC Champ — Video Script System

## Short-Form Script Formula

### 1. HOOK
Grab attention in the first few seconds.

### 2. CONTEXT
Explain what happened quickly.

### 3. STORY
Give the important details.

### 4. OPINION
Add the Real OTC Champ perspective.

### 5. PAYOFF
Give the viewer the key takeaway.

### 6. ENGAGEMENT
Ask a question that encourages comments.

### 7. CTA
Tell viewers to follow Real OTC Champ.
