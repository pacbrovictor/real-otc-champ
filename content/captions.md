## Caption Structure

1. Hook
2. What happened?
3. Why it matters
4. Opinion or analysis
5. Question to the audience
6. Call to action
7. Hashtags

## Engagement Questions

- Do you agree or disagree?
- Who do you think is right?
- Who should WWE push next?
- What would you do if you were booking WWE?
- Is WWE making the right decision?
- Who wins this matchup?
- What's your prediction?
- Rate this storyline from 1–10.
